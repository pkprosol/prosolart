# How to update your website

1. Go to prosolart.com/admin and sign in (use the email invite you received).
2. Pick the page on the left: Art, Architecture, About, Contact, or "Name, email, phone".

Add a painting
- Open "Art" → scroll to the bottom → "Add an entry".
- Click "Picture" → upload the photo straight from your phone or computer (any size is fine).
- Type the caption, e.g.  Sunset Field, 2026, 30"x40"
- Click "Save" (top right). The website updates in about 2 minutes.

Change the order
- Drag a painting up or down by the handle on its left. The top one shows first. Save.

Remove a painting
- Click the trash icon on that painting. Save.

Change the About text
- Open "About", edit like a normal document (bold, italic, links work). Save.

Made a mistake? Tell Peter — every save is kept and any version can be restored.
